What's new in 2.0.2

## Highlights

Write to us from the app
Settings > About > Send feedback.

Addresses and links kept as typed
The keyboard no longer corrects e-mail addresses or links.

No more invented endings
With Whisper, dictation ends on your last words.

## All changes

Write to us from the app
Settings > About > Send feedback, with the debug log if you want.

Addresses stay as you type them
The keyboard no longer autocorrects e-mail or web addresses.

No more invented words
With Whisper, dictations no longer end with "Thank you" or other words you never said.

Dictation on older iPhones
iPhone XS, XR, 11, SE (2nd generation) and iPads before the A14 dictate from the keyboard again, with Parakeet.

A microphone that tells you
If the microphone is slow to start, Dictus asks you to stay in the app.

History really cleared
Clear history removes all dictated text. Temporary copies are deleted after 7 days.
