What's new in 2.0.3

## Highlights

A redesigned keyboard
Keys sit where Apple's keyboard puts them, and a long press on the space bar moves the cursor.

Simpler emoji
Search, skin tones, and the full keyboard height for emoji.

Better French autocorrect
« il m'a », « à », accents and words typed together.

Redesigned keyboard
Keys placed like Apple's keyboard, on every iPhone, in portrait and landscape.

Cursor

Better French autocorrect
« il m'a », « à », accents, words typed together and apostrophes.

The Return key matches the field
Send, Go, Search.
