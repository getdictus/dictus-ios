Redesigned keyboard
Keys placed like Apple's keyboard, on every iPhone, in portrait and landscape.

Cursor
Long-press the space bar to move it.

New emoji keyboard
Search, skin tones, more room for emoji.

Better French autocorrect
« il m'a », « à », accents, words typed together and apostrophes.

The Return key matches the field
Send, Go, Search.

Text replacements
The ones in your iOS settings work everywhere.

Paste
One tap away in the keyboard bar.

WhatsApp
Autocorrect stays on after an @ mention.

Translation
Into 25 languages with « Other language… » (Dictus Pro, iPhone with Apple Intelligence).

Apple
Pick Apple's on-device dictation model in the Models screen.

Free trial
Available for Dictus Pro on every iPhone.
