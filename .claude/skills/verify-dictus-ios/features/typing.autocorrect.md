# Autocorrect and suggestions

- **ID:** `typing.autocorrect`
- **Tier:** Gratuit (Free)
- **Targets:** DictusKeyboard + DictusCore
- **Screens:** `kbd.keys`, `kbd.accent-popup`, `kbd.suggestions` (not captured) — see [screens.md](screens.md)

## Sub-features

- `typing.suggestions`: Suggestion bar with predictions and corrections.
- `typing.autocorrect-on-space`: A mistyped word is corrected when space is pressed. In French, after an elision (`d'` `l'` `j'` `m'` `n'` `t'` `s'` `c'` `qu'` `jusqu'` `lorsqu'` `puisqu'`) the correction never starts with a consonant other than h: `d'cirre` is kept rather than written `d'votre`, `l'homne` still gives `l'homme` (#1034).
- `typing.autocorrect-on-punctuation`: A mistyped word is also corrected by `, . ; : ! ?` and Return, never by `'` or `-` (#872). Never inside an e-mail address or a URL, whatever the field (#919): a token holding `@` or `://`, starting with `www.`, or with a `.` `:` `/` `_` glued to a letter, a digit or `/ _ @ + -` is left alone. The first segment, corrected at its `.` or `:`, is put back by the next key glued to the mark (`helene.` → `gelée.`, then `b` → `helene.b`).
- `typing.autocorrect-on-space`, French `a` or `à` (#501): a typed `a` becomes `à` when the words before prove a verb precedes it (`je vais a` → `je vais à`, `est arrivé a`, `du mal a`), and stays `a` after a subject, an object pronoun, a noun or inside a relative clause (`il a`, `il y a`, `le train a`, `ce que tu as fait a`). The first word of a sentence becomes `à`, capital kept (`A demain` → `À demain`, #979), but not a clause start after a comma. The suggestion bar previews `à` before space. French only.
- `typing.undo-correction`: The suggestion bar offers to undo the last correction.
- `typing.refused-correction`: Erasing into a correction, Undo, or tapping the quoted typed word refuses it: the word committed at that place next is kept and learned (#872). A text replacement shortcut is the exception: typed again at that place, it expands again (#1022).
- `typing.auto-full-stop`: Double space inserts a full stop.
- `typing.accents`: Long-press a letter for its accents. Each keyboard language has its own list, in the order Apple's keyboard for it shows them (#1042): French holds œ on `o` (`o ö ô œ ò ó õ ø ō º`) and æ on `a` (`a à â æ á ä ã å ā ª`), so `cœur` types by hand; shift or caps lock capitalizes them (Œ, Æ, ẞ). German on QWERTY or AZERTY holds ä ö ü first on a o u (QWERTZ has keys for them), Spanish holds ñ first on n. The French adaptive key (AZERTY row 3) is unchanged and holds nothing. A word typed with œ or æ is kept on space, capitals included (`cœur`, `Œil`, `vitæ`, `ex æquo`): the dictionaries spell the ligatures out (`coeur`), and the corrector looks a typed one up that way (`Ligature`, #1042). `coeur` typed with two letters is kept too, as before.
- `typing.dead-keys`: Dead keys compose accented letters.
- `typing.autocorrect-toggle`: « Correction automatique » switch in Réglages → Clavier.
- `typing.spatial-autocorrect`: test builds only (Debug, TestFlight), off by default (#1011). With the Experiments page's "Spatial autocorrect" switch on (App Group key `dictus.keyboard.spatialAutocorrect`, read at each keyboard appearance; ignored in the App Store build), the space bar reads where the finger landed on each key of the word (Hex's noisy-channel decoder, models `fr.hextm` and `en.hextm`) before the trie reads its letters: `trouce` with the `c` tapped on the edge of `v` becomes `trouve`, `jusge` becomes `juste`, a missed space splits (`donnequoi` → `donne quoi`). It runs after the lexicon, the overrides and the contractions, never on a learned word, and leaves the word to the trie when it keeps it. The Debug log prints `AUTOCORRECT branch=spatial`; each appearance logs `spatialAutocorrectConfigured on=… channel=… trigger=…`.
- `typing.adaptive-touch`: test builds only (Debug, TestFlight), off by default, and only with `typing.spatial-autocorrect` acting (#1014). With the Experiments page's "Adaptive touch" switch on (App Group key `dictus.keyboard.adaptiveTouch`, `KeyboardFeatureSettings.isAdaptiveTouchEffective`, read at each keyboard appearance with the spatial switch; ignored in the App Store build), the keyboard learns where this user lands on each letter key (Hex's Adaptive touch) from words that stood as typed, a text replacement shortcut that expanded (#1031), an undone correction, a same-length suggestion picked, a letter erased and retyped as a neighbour; never from a correction left in place, a slide, a popup accent, or a field where autocorrect does not run or that holds a secret. After 400 labelled taps over 3 sessions, while the profile fits recent taps better than the stock centres, the decoder reads the taps with those centres; a correction it puts in must still clear the stock centres' margin. Off, nothing is labelled or written and the decoder reads the stock centres. Each appearance logs `adaptiveTouchConfigured on=… trigger=…`. The averages (no text) are a 23 KB file in the App Group (`AdaptiveTouch/adaptive-touch.bin`); the Experiments page's « Reset adaptive touch » (`settings.keyboard.experiments.adaptiveTouchReset`, with a confirmation) and « Reset learned words » (`learned-words.reset`) delete it, and a running keyboard starts over at its next appearance.
- `typing.contacts-and-replacements`: Contact names and iOS text replacements (#873). A contact name or a phrase-only replacement is never corrected, a lowercase contact name takes its casing (`zofiak` → `Zofiak`), a shortcut is replaced by its phrase with the undo chip (`qqc` → `Quelque chose` at sentence start). Since #1022 a shortcut expands like on Apple's keyboard: with « Correction automatique » off, in a field that turns autocorrection off (search, URL, e-mail, username), for a shortcut holding a digit (`adr1`), and after any number of refusals at the same place. It never expands in a secure or password field, nor at the end of an e-mail address or a URL (#919; Apple's behaviour there is unchecked). Contact casing and curated names stay autocorrections: off when autocorrect is off.
- `typing.curated-names`: App and brand names (`CuratedNames`: WhatsApp, iPhone, YouTube…) are never corrected and take their casing when typed lowercase or with a sentence-start capital (`iphone` → `iPhone`). A misspelling corrected to one of them takes it too, in the bar and on space; all caps stays all caps. They are also correction targets the dictionaries do not hold (`CuratedNameCorrection`), in every language: the name minus its last letter (`iphon` → `iPhone`, `youtub` → `YouTube`, `whatsap` → `WhatsApp`, `l'iphon` → `l'iPhone`), or one other edit when no dictionary word is one edit away (`iphine` → `iPhone`; `ihone` stays `phone`). Never from a word the dictionary knows (`siphon`, `upon`, `youth`, `foutu`), from fewer than five letters, from two letters short (`youtu`), nor towards `Discord` or `Telegram`. A capitalized typo mid-sentence (`Iphon` after a word) is kept by the proper-noun guard.
- `typing.cursor-trackpad` (#950): Hold the space bar 0.35 s without sliding: the keys go blank and the space bar is a trackpad. The cursor follows the finger through the text in two dimensions, line to line, at the text's own scale, faster the faster the finger (Hex's model, MIT). Lifting types no space. A quick tap that drifts stays a space. A second finger types nothing. No haptic of its own. No selection: a keyboard cannot make one.
- `typing.delete-swipe` (#609): Press the delete key and slide left: each letter key's width deletes one more word, live, with one haptic tick per word (Hex's `DeleteWordSwipe`, MIT). Sliding back right types the words back, until the finger lifts; lifting keeps what is deleted. A word is what Option-Delete takes on a Mac, the spaces and punctuation after it included (`monde. `), with French rules: `l'homme`, `aujourd'hui`, `peut-être`, `12,5` are one word each, an emoji is a word of its own, a line break is a step of its own. The touch-down still deletes one character, and slid all the way back the slide gives that one back too. A tap and a stationary hold are unchanged (#419 cadence). The finger stays on delete for its whole touch: sliding over a letter no longer types it on lift. The slide stops editing at the first edit the proxy's mirror does not reflect (#530, logged `deleteSwipeEnded words=<n> frozen=true`). Réglages → Clavier → « Swipe to delete words » (`settings.keyboard.deleteWordSwipe`, on by default, every build) switched off makes delete the plain key again: no slide starts, the hold repeats, and no `deleteSwipeEnded` line is logged; read at each press.

## How to get to it (user POV)

1. Type with the Dictus keyboard in Notes or Messages.
2. Suggestions appear above the keys; a mistyped word is corrected when you press space.

## Driving it with axe + Argent

Measured 2026-10-20 (iPhone 17, iOS 26.5): a misspelling followed by space, typed through the Dictus keys in a Rappels field, is corrected.

```bash
D=.claude/skills/verify-dictus-ios/scripts/drive.sh
E=.claude/skills/verify-dictus-ios/scripts/evidence.sh
$D set-default dictus.autocorrectDebugLogging -bool YES   # Debug builds: logs each decision
$D full-access && $D select-keyboard                     # makes Dictus the current keyboard
xcrun simctl launch <udid> com.apple.reminders
$D tap-label Continuer                                   # first-run splash
$D tap-label "Plus tard"                                 # iCloud sync prompt
$D tap-label Rappel                                      # new reminder, title field focused
$D wait-id inputView 10
M=$($D log-mark)
$D type bonjoru                                          # the misspelling, key by key
$E typed-misspelling
$D space                                                 # autocorrect runs on space
$D wait-log 'APPLIED orig="Bonjoru" → "Bonjour"' 10 "$M"
$E after-space
argent run describe --udid <udid>                        # AXTextField "Titre" value="Bonjour "
```

The proof is two facts together: the field's value becomes `Bonjour ` and the log carries `APPLIED orig="Bonjoru" → "Bonjour"` after the mark. After the space the suggestion bar shows the undo chip `↶ Bonjoru` (`kbd.suggestions`); tapping it is `typing.undo-correction`. The first letter is capitalised by the host field, hence `Bonjoru`.

Measured 2026-10-30 (iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 27.0, build `fef07756`), keys tapped by coordinate in Messages: `la cuisinr,` → `La cuisine,` and `le bureai` + Return → `le bureau`, each followed by the undo chip; `la montagnr` → `montagne`, the word erased and retyped, logs `REFUSED corrected="montagne" by=backspace` then `AUTOCORRECT-SKIP word="montagnr" reason=refused-here`. The punctuation keys are on the `123` page.

Measured 2026-11-13 (iPhone 17, iOS 26.5, builds `fd92aaa0` and `bbaa7b36`, #919), in the Notes field of a Rappels item (tap under the title, « Ajouter une note »), every key tapped on the Dictus keys: `contact helene.bethmont@gmail.com site https://github.com/x et www.getdictus.com puis bonjoru. ` gives `Contact gelée.bethmont@émail.com site héros://github.com/x et wax.getdictus.com puis bonjour. ` before the fix and the text as typed, `bonjour.` aside, after it. The after log carries `ADDRESS-REVERT orig="helene" undone="gelée"`, `ADDRESS-REVERT orig="https" undone="héros"` and `AUTOCORRECT-SKIP word=… reason=address` for every later segment. `drive.sh type` has no punctuation; the keys were tapped at these points (iPhone 17, `inputView` at y = 548): letters rows y = 610 / 665 / 719 on the 40.2 pt pitch of `drive.sh`, `123` / `ABC` (25, 772), space (210, 772); on the `123` page `-` (20, 664), `/` (60, 664), `:` (100, 664), `@` (340, 664), `.` (104, 718), delete (371, 718). The keyboard stays on `123` after a mark, and goes back to letters by itself after `. ` and space.

Measured 2026-11-17 (iPhone 17, iOS 26.5, build `c9361321`, #501), same recipe: `je vais a elle a ` typed on the Dictus keys in a Rappels title reads `Je vais à elle a `, with `APPLIED orig="a" → "à" prev="vais"` then `AUTOCORRECT-SKIP word="a" reason=already-valid` in the log.

Measured 2026-11-24 (iPhone 17, iOS 26.5, #1042), Rappels title field: holding `o` on the French Dictus keys shows `o ö ô œ ò ó õ ø ō º` (`kbd.accent-popup`), `a` shows `a à â æ á ä ã å ā ª`, shifted `O Ö Ô Œ …` and `A À Â Æ …`. `c`, then `axe drag --start-x 340 --start-y 612 --end-x 144 --end-y 552 --hold 1.2 --duration 0.8` (from `o` onto `œ`), then `u`, `r` give `Cœur` in the field. To switch the Dictus language for a run, write `dictus.language` (`fr` `en` `es` `de`) into the App Group plist and reopen the host. Apple's own popups, the reference for the order, are read the same way with Apple's keyboard selected.

## Gotchas

- `typing.spatial-autocorrect`: turn it on by launching the Debug app with `-DictusDebugSpatialAutocorrect YES` (`xcrun simctl launch <udid> com.pivi.dictus -DictusDebugSpatialAutocorrect YES`, or `xcrun devicectl device process launch … com.pivi.dictus -DictusDebugSpatialAutocorrect YES` on the iPhone), with the switch on Réglages → Clavier → Expériences (`settings.keyboard.experiments.spatialAutocorrect`), or with `drive.sh set-default dictus.keyboard.spatialAutocorrect -bool YES`, then make the keyboard reappear. Until #1011's wiring fix the launch argument wrote `debug.spatialAutocorrect`, which the keyboard no longer reads. `simctl spawn defaults write group.solutions.pivi.dictus …` does not reach it: that domain is not the group container's plist. `drive.sh type` taps the key centres, which tells the decoder nothing the letters do not: its measure is `tools/keyboard-bench/run.sh --spatial` (synthetic and device-journal taps) and, for real fingers, the iPhone (Physical test shortlist).
- `typing.cursor-trackpad`: a multi-line field is needed, and a slimmed device has no Notes. A `<textarea>` page served from the Mac works in Safari (`python3 -m http.server --bind 127.0.0.1`, then `simctl openurl <udid> http://127.0.0.1:<port>/`; `simctl openurl` refuses `data:` URLs). `axe drag --hold 0.7 --start-x 210 --start-y 772 --end-x <x> --end-y <y> --duration 1 --steps 90` holds the space bar, then slides. Measured 2026-11-22 (iPhone 17, iOS 26.5), with Apple's keyboard on the same gestures as the reference: `docs/study-captures/trackpad/` on the `study/trackpad` branch. Safari shares about 60 characters around the cursor, cut anywhere, so a line whose start it has not shown is laid out by guess until it does. The feel, the speed curve on a real finger and the haptics are iPhone-only.

- `typing.adaptive-touch`: the switch is on Réglages → Clavier → Expériences (`settings.keyboard.experiments.adaptiveTouch`, greyed until « Spatial autocorrect » is on). `-DictusDebugAdaptiveTouch YES` on the Debug app's launch line writes the same key (`drive.sh set-default dictus.keyboard.adaptiveTouch -bool YES` too), `-DictusDebugAdaptiveTouchReset YES` forgets the profile; then make the keyboard reappear. `drive.sh type` taps the key centres, so the simulator never shows a bias to learn: its measure is `tools/keyboard-bench/run.sh --spatial --adaptive learn|frozen` with `--adaptive-load`/`--adaptive-save` (learn on one device session, score on another), and for a real thumb the iPhone.

- `typing.delete-swipe`: in a Rappels note (tap under the title, « Ajouter une note »), with the text already in the field. The slide is one held finger moved left then right without lifting: `axe serve`, then `touch -x 371 -y 719 --down --input-mode selected`, moves with `--contact-token`, and `--up` (axe 2.x; `axe draw` works too but keeps a constant speed, so no pause on a word). Start the slide within 0.5 s of the press, or the held repeat deletes characters first. One word per 40.2 pt on an iPhone 17. The log line `deleteSwipeEnded words=<n> frozen=false` is the proof, with the field's value. The haptic tick and how a real thumb finds the steps are iPhone-only. Measured 2026-11-24 (iPhone 17, iOS 26.5): Réglages → Général → Clavier rendered blank, so `drive.sh full-access` failed; Réglages → Apps → Dictus → Claviers showed the « Autoriser l’accès complet » switch, which a physical tap flipped. Also there: `axe touch --down --up` (legacy input) deleted two characters per backspace press and typed nothing on a letter, while `axe tap` and the selected-input touch deleted one; drive presses with those.
- Safari's address bar turns suggestions off: typing there shows no suggestion bar. Test in Rappels. A slimmed iOS 26.5 device has no Notes app (`simctl launch com.apple.mobilenotes` fails), and on 2026-11-13 Messages opened a conversation without drawing its compose box.
- Reset the user dictionary first (`typing.learned-words`): a learned word silently changes what autocorrect does.
- `axe type` does not exercise autocorrect: it sends hardware keyboard events that reach the field without going through the extension. `drive.sh type` taps the Dictus keys instead (lowercase a–z, AZERTY letters page, offsets measured on iPhone 17).
- `typing.contacts-and-replacements`: create the contact in Contacts and the replacement in Réglages → Général → Clavier → Remplacement, then make the keyboard reappear: the lexicon is read in `viewWillAppear`. The Debug log prints `NOTE lexicon loaded: <n> shortcuts, <n> words`, then `AUTOCORRECT-SKIP ... reason=lexicon` or `AUTOCORRECT branch=lexicon`. Measured 2026-10-30 on iOS 27.0 (#873).
- `typing.contacts-and-replacements` with autocorrect off: write `dictus.autocorrectEnabled -bool NO` and `dictus.autocorrectDebugLogging -bool YES` into the App Group plist, type the shortcut and space. The log carries `AUTOCORRECT branch=text-replacement`, a line written only when autocorrect did not run. Measured 2026-11-21 (iPhone 17, iOS 26.5, #1022) in a Rappels title, without Full Access: `jrv` + space → `J’arrive ! `. `jrv` → « J’arrive ! » is iOS's own French replacement, present on a fresh device. On that runtime Réglages → Général → Clavier stayed blank, slimmed or not (Settings logs `PreferencesPluginError`), so no replacement could be added by hand that day.
- `typing.accents`: the popup selects only once the finger moves onto an option; a hold released where it started types nothing (vendored behaviour). `axe batch` touch down / up therefore never inserts: use `axe drag --hold`. The first hold after the keyboard reappears or a language switch sometimes shows the key preview without a popup (seen twice on 2026-11-24); hold again. The feel of a long row of narrowed options under a real finger is iPhone-only.
- Host spell-check underlines are not a usable probe in the simulator (`docs/agents/simulator.md` §8).
- A refusal is remembered per place in the current document, in RAM, until the text before it is erased. Clearing a field by backspace forgets it; the field emptied by the host (a sent message) may not, if the host keeps the same `documentIdentifier`.
